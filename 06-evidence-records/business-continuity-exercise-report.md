## 2. Exercise Objectives

The exercise is intended to assess:

- Business continuity plan awareness.
- Incident escalation and activation procedures.
- Management decision-making.
- Recovery prioritisation.
- RTO and RPO awareness.
- Stakeholder communication.
- Security considerations during recovery.
- Technical and business recovery validation requirements.

## 3. Scenario Summary

The exercise scenario involves a simulated AWS infrastructure disruption affecting CloudLedger's production application and database services.

The disruption prevents customers from accessing the SaaS financial management platform.

Participants are expected to discuss how they would identify the disruption, activate continuity arrangements, coordinate recovery and communicate with affected stakeholders.

No production systems will be affected.
