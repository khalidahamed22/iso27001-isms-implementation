## 1. Assessment Purpose

This questionnaire evaluates the information security practices of a third-party supplier that provides services to CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

The assessment identifies potential security gaps and risks associated with supplier services, access to information, infrastructure, data processing and service availability.
