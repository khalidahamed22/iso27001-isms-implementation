## 6. Lessons Learned

The following lessons are proposed for validation through an incident response exercise:

1. Employees should have a clear and accessible method for reporting suspicious emails.
2. Security teams should preserve relevant evidence before taking actions that could alter it.
3. Incident severity should be reassessed as new evidence becomes available.
4. Technical and management escalation responsibilities should be clear.
5. Corrective actions should have assigned owners, target dates and closure evidence.

## 7. Review Conclusion

The portfolio exercise demonstrates the documentation structure required to record and review a suspected phishing incident.

However, the incident investigation, containment, recovery and technical response effectiveness have not been demonstrated. Therefore, no conclusion can be made about the actual effectiveness of incident response controls.

The incident remains open in the simulated incident register.
