## 7. Management Recommendations

Management should:

- Continue the annual awareness programme.
- Ensure role-specific training is provided.
- Monitor overdue training assignments.
- Evaluate knowledge gaps and repeated assessment failures.
- Review phishing reporting behaviour and incident trends.
- Allocate resources for awareness improvements.

## 8. Conclusion

The simulated assessment data demonstrates how training effectiveness can be measured using assessment scores, pass rates and remediation tracking.

However, quiz results alone do not establish behavioural improvement or effective security awareness.

Further evaluation through practical exercises, employee feedback and security incident trends is recommended.
