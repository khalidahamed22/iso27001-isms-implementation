# Business Continuity Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-005  
**Organisation:** CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd  
**Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Policy Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approval Authority:** Senior Management  
**Review Frequency:** Annually or following significant business changes

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the principles and management requirements for maintaining critical business operations during disruptive events.

CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd recognises that disruptions involving technology, people, facilities, suppliers or cyber incidents may affect its ability to deliver services to customers.

The organisation is committed to establishing, maintaining and continually improving appropriate business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees and relevant contractors.
- Business processes supporting the CloudLedger SaaS platform.
- AWS cloud infrastructure and associated services.
- Customer financial information and supporting databases.
- Critical third-party service providers.
- Business operations conducted from the Colombo office and approved remote-working locations.

## 3. Policy Objectives

The organisation aims to:

1. Identify critical business processes and supporting dependencies.
2. Assess the business impact of operational disruptions.
3. Establish appropriate recovery priorities and objectives.
4. Maintain documented business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements.
5. Protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information during disruptions.
6. Define responsibilities for incident escalation and recovery.
7. Test recovery arrangements periodically.
8. Review and improve continuity capabilities based on test results and lessons learned.

## 4. Management Commitment

Senior management shall:

- Provide appropriate resources for business continuity planning.
- Approve business recovery priorities and relevant recovery objectives.
- Assign accountable owners for critical business processes.
- Review significant continuity risks.
- Support periodic testing and improvement activities.
- Ensure continuity arrangements are consistent with business and contractual requirements.

## 5. Business Impact Analysis

The organisation shall maintain a Business Impact Analysis (BIA) to:

- Identify critical business processes.
- Assess financial, operational, legal, reputational and information security impacts.
- Identify dependencies on technology, personnel, facilities and external providers.
- Determine Maximum Tolerable Periods of Disruption (MTPD).
- Establish proposed Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).

BIA results shall be reviewed with relevant process owners and approved by management.

## 6. Business Continuity Planning

Business continuity plans shall address:

- Disruption identification and escalation.
- Business continuity activation criteria.
- Roles and responsibilities.
- Alternative working arrangements.
- Internal and external communication.
- Customer and supplier coordination.
- Prioritisation of critical business activities.
- Recovery and return to normal operations.

## 7. Disaster Recovery

The organisation shall maintain appropriate disaster recovery arrangements for critical information systems and technology services.

These arrangements shall address:

- Cloud infrastructure recovery.
- Application and database restoration.
- Backup availability and integrity.
- Identity and access service recovery.
- Network and connectivity dependencies.
- Security monitoring and logging restoration.
- Recovery validation and business acceptance.

Recovery targets shall be established based on business requirements and demonstrated technical capabilities.

## 8. Backup and Information Protection

Backup and recovery arrangements shall follow the approved Backup and Recovery Procedure.

Backups shall be appropriately protected against unauthorised access, modification, deletion and other threats.

Restoration activities shall include appropriate checks to establish data integrity and service functionality.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Senior Management | Approve continuity policy, priorities and resources |
| Information Security Manager | Coordinate security-related continuity requirements |
| IT Manager | Coordinate technology recovery activities |
| Cloud Administrator | Support cloud infrastructure and system recovery |
| Head of Engineering | Coordinate application recovery and technical validation |
| Business Process Owners | Identify business requirements and validate recovery |
| Employees | Follow continuity instructions and report disruptions |
| Third-Party Providers | Support agreed recovery responsibilities under contractual arrangements |

## 10. Third-Party Dependencies

Critical suppliers and service providers shall be considered during continuity planning.

The organisation shall:

- Identify critical external dependencies.
- Assess supplier-related continuity risks.
- Review relevant contractual recovery commitments.
- Understand shared responsibilities for cloud services.
- Consider alternative arrangements where appropriate.
- Obtain and review supplier continuity assurance where available.

## 11. Testing and Exercises

Business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements shall be tested at planned intervals and following significant changes where appropriate.

Testing may include:

- Tabletop exercises.
- Backup restoration tests.
- Application recovery exercises.
- Cloud infrastructure recovery tests.
- Communication and escalation exercises.

Test results, identified weaknesses and corrective actions shall be documented.

## 12. Training and Awareness

Relevant employees shall receive appropriate awareness and instructions regarding their continuity responsibilities.

Personnel assigned recovery responsibilities shall have the competence required to perform their assigned duties.

## 13. Policy Exceptions

Any exception to this policy shall:

- Be documented with a business justification.
- Include an assessment of associated risks.
- Identify compensating measures where appropriate.
- Be reviewed and approved by an authorised management representative.
- Have an agreed review or expiry date.

## 14. Compliance and Review

Compliance with this policy shall be reviewed through appropriate monitoring, internal audits, management reviews and continuity exercises.

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually or when significant organisational, technological, contractual or regulatory changes occur.
