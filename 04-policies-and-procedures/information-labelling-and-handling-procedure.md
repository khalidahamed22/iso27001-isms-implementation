
# Information Labelling and Handling Procedure

**Document ID:** PROC-003  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Process Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document  
**Effective Date:** Pending Approval  
**Classification:** Internal  

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the operational steps for applying information classification labels and handling requirements throughout the information lifecycle at CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to employees, contractors and authorised third parties handling CloudLedger information in electronic or physical formats.

## 3. Information Classification Levels

| Classification | Examples | Minimum Handling Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Published website content | Approved for public disclosure |
| Internal | Internal procedures | Authorised internal access |
| Confidential | Employee records and supplier contracts | Restricted access and approved secure sharing |
| Restricted | Customer financial records and authentication secrets | Explicit authorisation, strong access restrictions and protected storage and transmission |

## 4. Classification Process

### Step 1 – Identify Information

The information owner identifies the information being created, received, processed or stored.

### Step 2 – Determine Classification

The information owner evaluates:

- Confidentiality requirements.
- Integrity requirements.
- Availability requirements.
- Legal and regulatory obligations.
- Contractual commitments.
- Business impact if information is disclosed, modified, lost or unavailable.

### Step 3 – Assign Classification

The information owner assigns one of the four approved classification levels.

The classification must be recorded in the Information Classification Register where the information represents a relevant information asset or information category.

### Step 4 – Apply the Label

Where technically supported, the classification must be displayed in the document header, footer, file metadata or approved system label.

### Step 5 – Apply Handling Requirements

Employees must follow the handling requirements associated with the assigned classification.

## 5. Information Handling Workflow

### 5.1 Creating Information

- Employees must identify the information owner.
- Information must receive an appropriate classification.
- Documents must be labelled where practical.
- Sensitive information must be created and stored using approved systems.

### 5.2 Storing Information

- Public and Internal information may be stored in approved business repositories.
- Confidential information must be stored in access-controlled repositories.
- Restricted information must be stored in approved systems with strong access restrictions and appropriate technical safeguards.
- Information must not be copied to unauthorised personal storage services.

### 5.3 Sharing Information

Before sharing information, employees must:

1. Confirm the recipient's identity.
2. Verify that the recipient has a legitimate business need.
3. Obtain required approval from the information owner.
4. Use an approved sharing method.
5. Apply appropriate access restrictions.
6. Confirm that access is limited to the intended recipient.

Restricted information must not be shared externally without explicit authorisation and appropriate protection.

### 5.4 Transmitting Information

- Use approved secure communication channels.
- Verify recipients before sending sensitive information.
- Apply encryption where required by risk assessment, legal obligations or contractual requirements.
- Do not send authentication secrets through ordinary email or chat.
- Report accidental disclosure immediately through the incident management process.

### 5.5 Printing and Physical Handling

- Collect printed Confidential and Restricted documents immediately.
- Do not leave sensitive documents unattended in public areas.
- Store physical records in secure cabinets or controlled areas.
- Dispose of sensitive printed material using approved secure destruction methods.

### 5.6 Reclassification

Information owners must review classifications when:

- Business requirements change.
- Information becomes publicly available through an authorised release.
- Legal or contractual requirements change.
- Information sensitivity changes.
- The original classification is no longer appropriate.

Reclassification decisions must be documented.

### 5.7 Information Disposal

- Confirm that the information is no longer required.
- Check applicable retention and legal requirements.
- Obtain required approval.
- Use secure deletion or destruction methods appropriate to the storage medium.
- Retain disposal evidence where required.

## 6. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Information Owner | Assigns and reviews classifications |
| Information Security Manager | Provides guidance and monitors compliance |
| IT Administrator | Implements technical handling and access safeguards |
| Employees | Follow classification and handling procedures |
| HR Manager | Ensures employee awareness and acknowledgement |

## 7. Records to Maintain

The following records should be retained:

- Information Classification Register.
- Information reclassification records.
- External information-sharing approvals.
- Information disposal records.
- Security incident reports involving information disclosure.
- Employee security awareness records.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, risk-assessed and approved by the appropriate information owner before the activity takes place, except where emergency procedures apply.
