## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes security requirements for identifying, assessing, selecting, approving, monitoring and managing third-party suppliers that provide services to CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

The objective is to protect company information and services against risks arising from external supplier relationships.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Cloud infrastructure providers.
- Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) providers.
- Payment service providers.
- Managed IT and security service providers.
- Software development and technology partners.
- Data processing providers.
- Suppliers with access to CloudLedger information or systems.

## 3. Supplier Security Principles

CloudLedger shall:

- Assess supplier security risks before engagement.
- Apply security requirements proportionate to supplier risk.
- Include relevant security obligations in supplier agreements.
- Restrict supplier access to authorised business requirements.
- Monitor supplier security performance.
- Review supplier risks periodically.
- Manage supplier changes and termination securely.

## 4. Supplier Risk Classification

Suppliers shall be classified according to their access to information, service criticality and potential business impact.

| Supplier Risk Level | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Supports essential production services or processes Restricted information | AWS production infrastructure provider |
| High | Processes Confidential or Restricted information or provides important business services | Payment processing provider |
| Medium | Provides business services with limited sensitive information access | Customer support SaaS provider |
| Low | Provides services with no significant access to company information or critical systems | General office supplies provider |

The classification must be based on an assessment of actual services and access, not solely on the supplier's name or industry.

## 5. Supplier Due Diligence

Before a supplier is approved, the responsible business owner and relevant security personnel shall assess:

- Supplier security policies and governance.
- Information protection and access controls.
- Data storage and processing locations.
- Encryption and key management practices.
- Vulnerability and patch management.
- Incident notification and response arrangements.
- Business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Subcontractor and fourth-party dependencies.
- Regulatory and contractual obligations.
- Independent assurance reports or certifications, where available and relevant.

Identified risks must be documented and addressed before approval or accepted through the appropriate risk acceptance process.

## 6. Supplier Agreements

Supplier agreements shall include relevant requirements such as:

- Confidentiality obligations.
- Data protection responsibilities.
- Information security requirements.
- Access restrictions.
- Incident notification obligations.
- Data retention and deletion requirements.
- Audit or assurance rights, where appropriate.
- Business continuity expectations.
- Subcontractor requirements.
- Contract termination and data return arrangements.

## 7. Cloud Service Security

Cloud service providers shall be assessed for:

- Shared responsibility arrangements.
- Identity and access management.
- Data protection and encryption.
- Logging and monitoring capabilities.
- Backup and recovery capabilities.
- Service availability commitments.
- Security incident responsibilities.
- Data location and deletion arrangements.

CloudLedger remains responsible for managing security risks arising from its use of cloud services, including responsibilities retained under the applicable shared responsibility model.

## 8. Supplier Access Management

- Supplier access must be formally authorised.
- Access must follow least-privilege principles.
- MFA must be applied where supported and appropriate.
- Supplier accounts must be individually attributable.
- Privileged access must be restricted and monitored.
- Access must be removed when no longer required.
- Supplier access must be reviewed periodically.

## 9. Supplier Monitoring and Review

Supplier security performance and risk shall be reviewed according to risk level.

Reviews may consider:

- Changes in supplier services.
- Security incidents and breaches.
- Assurance reports and certifications.
- Service availability and performance.
- Contractual compliance.
- Changes to subcontractors.
- Changes in data processing locations.
- Corrective actions and unresolved security findings.

Critical and high-risk suppliers should receive more frequent scrutiny than low-risk suppliers.

## 10. Supplier Changes and Termination

Before terminating or materially changing a supplier relationship, CloudLedger shall consider:

- Revocation of supplier access.
- Return or secure deletion of company information.
- Transfer of business-critical information.
- Replacement service arrangements.
- Continuity and recovery risks.
- Contractual termination obligations.

## 11. Exceptions and Risk Acceptance

Supplier security exceptions must be documented, assessed and approved by authorised management and the relevant risk owner.

Risk acceptance must consider legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.
