
# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes management direction and organisational requirements for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information handled by CloudLedger Technologies.

It provides the foundation for the organisation's Information Security Management System (ISMS).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees and management.
- Contractors and temporary personnel.
- Third-party users with authorised access.
- Information assets within the ISMS scope.
- Cloud infrastructure and business applications.
- Information created, processed, transmitted or stored by the organisation.

## 3. Information Security Objectives

CloudLedger is committed to:

1. Protecting customer, employee and business information.
2. Preventing unauthorised access, disclosure, modification and destruction of information.
3. Identifying and managing information security risks.
4. Maintaining compliance with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.
5. Establishing clear information security responsibilities.
6. Maintaining business continuity and information recovery capabilities.
7. Continually improving the effectiveness of the ISMS.

## 4. Management Commitment

Executive Management shall:

- Support the establishment and maintenance of the ISMS.
- Provide appropriate resources for information security activities.
- Approve the Information Security Policy.
- Ensure information security responsibilities are assigned.
- Review ISMS performance and significant risks.
- Support continual improvement.

## 5. Information Security Responsibilities

### Executive Management

- Provide strategic direction and oversight.
- Approve security policies and risk acceptance decisions within their authority.
- Review significant security incidents and risks.

### Information Security Manager

- Coordinate ISMS activities.
- Maintain security policies and procedures.
- Coordinate risk assessments and internal audits.
- Monitor security compliance and performance.

### IT Manager

- Maintain secure IT infrastructure.
- Manage access controls and technical safeguards.
- Coordinate backup, recovery and vulnerability management.

### Department Managers

- Ensure their teams follow applicable security requirements.
- Review access rights and report security concerns.
- Support security awareness activities.

### Employees and Contractors

- Follow approved security policies.
- Protect organisational information and credentials.
- Use systems only for authorised business purposes.
- Report suspected security incidents promptly.

## 6. Information Classification and Handling

Information must be classified according to its sensitivity and business value.

The organisation uses:

- Public.
- Internal.
- Confidential.
- Restricted.

Information owners are responsible for determining appropriate classification and handling requirements.

## 7. Access Control

Access to information and systems must:

- Be based on legitimate business requirements.
- Follow the principle of least privilege.
- Require authorised approval.
- Be reviewed periodically.
- Be revoked or adjusted when responsibilities change or employment ends.

Privileged access must receive additional protection and monitoring.

## 8. Asset Management

Information assets must be identified, assigned owners and maintained in an inventory.

Employees must protect assigned assets and return company property when required.

## 9. Supplier and Cloud Security

Third-party suppliers must be assessed according to their information security risks.

Cloud services must be used in accordance with approved security requirements, contractual responsibilities and the organisation's cloud security procedures.

## 10. Incident Management

Suspected information security incidents must be reported through approved reporting channels.

The organisation shall maintain procedures for:

- Incident identification.
- Initial assessment and classification.
- Containment and response.
- Investigation and evidence collection.
- Recovery.
- Lessons learned and corrective actions.

## 11. Business Continuity and Backup

Critical information and systems must have appropriate backup and recovery arrangements.

Recovery requirements shall be determined according to business impact and continuity needs.

## 12. Security Awareness

Employees and relevant contractors must receive information security awareness training appropriate to their responsibilities.

## 13. Compliance

Violations of this policy may result in corrective or disciplinary action in accordance with applicable organisational procedures and legal requirements.

Applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations must be identified and monitored.

## 14. Policy Exceptions

Exceptions must:

- Be formally documented.
- Include a business justification.
- Identify associated risks.
- Include compensating controls where appropriate.
- Receive approval from an authorised risk owner.
- Have an expiry or review date.

## 15. Policy Review

The Information Security Manager shall review this policy at least annually and following significant changes to business operations, technology, threats or applicable obligations.
