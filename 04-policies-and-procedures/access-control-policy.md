## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for managing and controlling access to CloudLedger Technologies' information, systems, applications, infrastructure and business resources.

The objective is to ensure that access is granted only to authorised individuals and is appropriate to their job responsibilities and business requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors and temporary personnel.
- Third-party users who access CloudLedger resources.
- Cloud infrastructure and hosted environments.
- Business applications and databases.
- Corporate networks and endpoints.
- Source code repositories and development environments.
- Customer information and confidential business records.
- Privileged and administrative accounts.

## 3. Access Control Principles

CloudLedger shall follow these principles:

### 3.1 Least Privilege

Users shall receive only the minimum access permissions necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

### 3.2 Need-to-Know

Access to confidential and restricted information shall be limited to individuals with a legitimate business requirement.

### 3.3 Role-Based Access Control

Access permissions should be assigned based on defined job roles and responsibilities wherever practical.

### 3.4 Segregation of Duties

Conflicting responsibilities shall be separated where reasonably possible to reduce fraud, errors and unauthorised activities.

### 3.5 Individual Accountability

Users shall be assigned unique accounts wherever technically feasible. Sharing personal accounts or authentication credentials is prohibited.

## 4. User Access Management

All access to company systems must follow an approved access request process.

Access requests shall:

- Identify the individual requesting access.
- Specify the system or resource.
- Identify the required permissions.
- Include a business justification.
- Receive approval from the relevant manager and system owner, as applicable.
- Be implemented by authorised IT personnel.

Access must not be granted solely on an informal verbal request unless an approved emergency process applies.

## 5. Authentication Requirements

CloudLedger shall implement appropriate authentication safeguards.

Requirements include:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for privileged accounts and remote access.
- MFA for other systems where supported and justified by risk.
- Strong authentication mechanisms for sensitive applications.
- Secure storage and handling of authentication credentials.
- Prohibition of password sharing.
- Immediate reporting of suspected credential compromise.
- Secure account recovery and password reset processes.

## 6. Privileged Access Management

Privileged access shall be restricted to authorised personnel who require administrative permissions.

The organisation shall:

- Maintain an inventory of privileged accounts.
- Require approval before granting privileged access.
- Use separate administrative accounts where appropriate.
- Apply MFA to privileged accounts.
- Monitor privileged activities where technically feasible.
- Review privileged access periodically.
- Remove unnecessary administrative permissions promptly.

Shared administrative accounts should be avoided. Where technically unavoidable, compensating controls and accountability mechanisms must be implemented.

## 7. Joiner, Mover and Leaver Management

CloudLedger shall maintain a formal process for managing access throughout the employment lifecycle.

### 7.1 Joiners

When a new employee or contractor joins:

- HR or the relevant responsible department shall notify IT.
- The employee's manager shall request required access.
- Access shall be approved before provisioning.
- IT shall create accounts and assign appropriate permissions.
- Users shall receive relevant security instructions.

### 7.2 Movers

When an employee changes roles or responsibilities:

- The manager shall request changes to existing permissions.
- Access no longer required shall be removed.
- New permissions shall be approved.
- Conflicting access rights shall be reviewed.

### 7.3 Leavers

When an employee or contractor leaves:

- HR shall notify IT of the termination or departure.
- Access shall be disabled or removed in accordance with the approved offboarding process and departure timing.
- Privileged credentials and shared secrets shall be rotated where necessary.
- Company devices and assets shall be returned.
- Access removal shall be recorded and verified.

Urgent or involuntary departures shall receive immediate access revocation coordinated with HR and management.

## 8. User Access Reviews

System owners and relevant managers shall periodically review user access permissions.

Reviews shall verify:

- Whether each user still requires access.
- Whether permissions remain appropriate for current job responsibilities.
- Whether inactive or unnecessary accounts exist.
- Whether privileged access remains justified.
- Whether terminated employees retain active access.

Review results and remediation actions shall be documented.

## 9. Remote Access

Remote access to CloudLedger resources shall be authorised and protected through appropriate security controls.

These may include:

- MFA.
- Approved secure remote access solutions.
- Encryption of data in transit.
- Endpoint security requirements.
- Access restrictions based on business needs.
- Monitoring of remote access activities.

## 10. Third-Party Access

Third-party access shall be granted only when required for approved business purposes.

The organisation shall:

- Obtain appropriate authorisation.
- Define access scope and duration.
- Apply least privilege.
- Ensure relevant contractual security requirements are established.
- Monitor access where appropriate.
- Revoke access when the engagement ends or the business requirement ceases.

## 11. Emergency Access

Emergency access may be granted when necessary to restore critical services or address urgent security incidents.

Such access must:

- Be limited to the emergency requirement.
- Be authorised by an appropriate responsible person where practicable.
- Be logged and monitored.
- Be reviewed after the emergency.
- Be revoked when no longer required.

## 12. Access Violations

Suspected unauthorised access, credential sharing, misuse of privileges or access control violations must be reported through the organisation's incident reporting process.

Violations shall be investigated and addressed according to applicable disciplinary and incident management procedures.

## 13. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require documented business justification, risk assessment and approval from the Information Security Manager or authorised management representative.

Exceptions shall be time-limited and reviewed.

## 14. Compliance and Monitoring

CloudLedger shall monitor access control arrangements through:

- Access reviews.
- Privileged account reviews.
- Authentication logs.
- Account lifecycle records.
- Security monitoring.
- Internal audits.
- Corrective action tracking.

## 15. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and following significant changes to business operations, technology, legal requirements or information security risks.
