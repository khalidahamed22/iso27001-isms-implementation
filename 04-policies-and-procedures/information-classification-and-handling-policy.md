
# Information Classification and Handling Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for classifying, labelling, handling, storing, transmitting, retaining and securely disposing of information belonging to CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

The objective is to ensure that information receives protection appropriate to its confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors and authorised third parties.
- Customer and employee information.
- Financial and transaction records.
- Application source code and technical documentation.
- Authentication credentials and security information.
- Cloud infrastructure and business systems.
- Paper records and electronic information.

## 3. Information Classification Levels

CloudLedger uses the following four classification levels.

| Classification | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Information approved for unrestricted public disclosure | Published marketing materials and public website content |
| Internal | Routine business information intended for authorised personnel | Internal procedures and general operational documents |
| Confidential | Sensitive business information requiring restricted access | Internal security reports, supplier contracts and employee records |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access restrictions | Customer financial data, authentication secrets and sensitive personal information |

## 4. Classification Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Information Owner | Determines classification and approves access requirements |
| Asset Owner | Ensures information assets receive appropriate protection |
| Information Custodian | Implements technical and operational safeguards |
| Information Security Manager | Provides classification guidance and monitors compliance |
| Employees | Follow classification and handling requirements |

## 5. Information Handling Requirements

### 5.1 Public Information

- Public information may be shared externally after appropriate approval.
- Employees must not publish internal information without authorisation.
- Public information must remain accurate and approved for release.

### 5.2 Internal Information

- Access is limited to employees and authorised parties with a business need.
- Information must be stored in approved company systems.
- External sharing requires appropriate authorisation.

### 5.3 Confidential Information

- Access must be restricted to authorised personnel.
- Information must be stored in approved protected repositories.
- External transmission must use approved secure communication methods.
- Printed copies must be protected against unauthorised access.
- Information must not be stored on unapproved personal devices.

### 5.4 Restricted Information

- Access must be explicitly authorised by the information owner.
- Strong authentication and least-privilege access must be applied.
- Encryption must be used where required by risk assessment, contractual obligations or applicable legal requirements.
- Sensitive information must only be transmitted through approved protected channels.
- Access must be logged and periodically reviewed.
- Unauthorised copying, downloading or disclosure is prohibited.

## 6. Information Labelling

Where practical, information must be labelled according to its classification.

Examples:

- PUBLIC
- INTERNAL
- CONFIDENTIAL
- RESTRICTED

Electronic documents should display the classification in the document header or metadata where supported.

## 7. Information Storage

- Information must be stored in approved company systems.
- Access permissions must reflect information classification.
- Restricted information must not be stored in unauthorised personal cloud accounts.
- Backups must receive protection appropriate to the information they contain.
- Storage locations must be reviewed periodically.

## 8. Information Transmission and Sharing

- Employees must verify recipients before sharing sensitive information.
- Confidential and Restricted information must use approved secure transmission methods.
- Access links must be restricted to intended recipients.
- Public file-sharing links must not be used for Confidential or Restricted information unless explicitly approved and appropriately protected.

## 9. Retention and Disposal

- Information must be retained according to approved retention requirements.
- Information that is no longer required must be securely deleted or disposed of.
- Storage media must be sanitised before re-use or disposal.
- Legal, regulatory and contractual retention obligations must be considered before deletion.

## 10. Exceptions

Exceptions must be:

- Documented with a business justification.
- Assessed for information security risk.
- Approved by the appropriate information owner.
- Reviewed periodically.
- Supported by appropriate risk treatment measures.

## 11. Policy Compliance

Violations may result in access restrictions, investigation, disciplinary action or contractual consequences, subject to applicable company procedures and law.

## 12. Review and Approval

This policy must be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in business operations, technology, legal requirements or information security risks.
