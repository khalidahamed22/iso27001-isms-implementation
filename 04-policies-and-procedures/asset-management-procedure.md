# Information Asset Management Procedure

**Document ID:** CL-PROC-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Document Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document  
**Process Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Supporting Functions:** IT Operations, Department Managers, Asset Owners, HR  
**Approval Authority:** Executive Management  
**Review Frequency:** Annually or following significant changes

## 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes how CloudLedger identifies, records, assigns ownership, classifies, maintains and disposes of information assets.

The objective is to ensure that information assets are accounted for and protected according to their business value, sensitivity and information security risks.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to information assets owned, managed, processed or used by CloudLedger, including:

- Physical IT equipment.
- Servers and cloud infrastructure.
- Applications and databases.
- Customer and employee information.
- Source code and development resources.
- Network infrastructure.
- Backups and storage media.
- Third-party services supporting business operations.
- Information processing facilities.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Management | Provide oversight and approve relevant resources |
| Information Security Manager | Define asset management requirements and monitor compliance |
| Asset Owner | Ensure assigned assets are appropriately protected |
| IT Operations | Maintain technical asset records and configurations |
| Department Managers | Identify departmental information assets |
| Employees | Protect assigned assets and report loss or damage |
| Procurement | Record purchased equipment and supplier details |

## 4. Asset Identification

Departments shall identify information assets used, created, stored or processed within their business activities.

Assets shall be recorded in the central asset inventory.

Each asset should have:

- Unique asset identifier.
- Asset name.
- Asset category.
- Description.
- Assigned owner.
- Location or hosting environment.
- Information classification, where applicable.
- Confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.
- Relevant dependencies.
- Lifecycle status.

The inventory shall be updated when assets are created, acquired, modified, transferred or disposed of.

## 5. Asset Ownership

Every information asset shall have an assigned owner.

Asset owners are responsible for:

- Determining appropriate information classification.
- Identifying business and security requirements.
- Authorising access where applicable.
- Reviewing asset-related risks.
- Ensuring appropriate protection measures are implemented.
- Reviewing asset information periodically.
- Authorising disposal or transfer where applicable.

Technical administration of an asset does not automatically make the IT administrator its business owner.

## 6. Information Classification

Information assets shall be classified according to their sensitivity and business impact.

CloudLedger uses the following classification levels:

| Classification | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Approved for public disclosure | Public website content |
| Internal | Intended for internal business use | Internal procedures |
| Confidential | Sensitive business or customer information | Employee records and business contracts |
| Restricted | Highly sensitive information requiring strict access restrictions | Authentication secrets and sensitive financial records |

Asset owners shall determine the appropriate classification based on business requirements, legal obligations and potential consequences of unauthorised disclosure or modification.

## 7. Asset Handling and Protection

Assets shall be protected according to their classification and identified risks.

Protection requirements may include:

- Access restrictions.
- Encryption.
- Secure storage.
- Backup and recovery.
- Endpoint protection.
- Physical security.
- Logging and monitoring.
- Secure transmission.
- Secure disposal.

Employees must not transfer confidential or restricted information to unauthorised locations or services.

## 8. Hardware and Equipment Management

IT Operations shall maintain records of company-owned hardware.

Records should include:

- Asset ID.
- Device type.
- Manufacturer and model.
- Serial number.
- Assigned employee or department.
- Location.
- Acquisition date.
- Warranty information where relevant.
- Current lifecycle status.

Equipment issued to employees shall be recorded and acknowledged.

Lost, stolen or damaged equipment must be reported promptly through the incident reporting process.

## 9. Cloud and Virtual Assets

CloudLedger shall maintain visibility of its cloud-hosted resources and relevant dependencies.

Cloud assets may include:

- Virtual machines.
- Storage services.
- Managed databases.
- Network resources.
- Cloud identities and service accounts.
- Backup resources.
- Security monitoring services.

Cloud resource ownership, purpose and security responsibilities shall be documented where appropriate.

Changes to cloud resources shall follow the applicable change management process.

## 10. Asset Transfer and Return

Asset transfers between employees, departments or locations shall be authorised and recorded.

When an employee leaves the organisation:

- Assigned equipment shall be returned.
- Asset records shall be updated.
- Company information shall be protected.
- Access to company resources shall be revoked through the JML process.
- Returned equipment shall be inspected before reassignment.

## 11. Asset Disposal

Assets shall be disposed of securely when they are no longer required.

Before disposal:

1. Obtain appropriate disposal authorisation.
2. Identify information stored on the asset.
3. Apply appropriate data sanitisation or destruction methods.
4. Verify sanitisation where practicable.
5. Use approved disposal providers where necessary.
6. Update the asset inventory.
7. Retain disposal records.

Disposal methods shall be selected according to asset type, information sensitivity, legal requirements and applicable technical guidance.

## 12. Asset Inventory Review

Asset owners and IT Operations shall periodically review the asset inventory.

Reviews shall identify:

- Missing or unassigned assets.
- Incorrect ownership information.
- Unregistered cloud resources.
- Assets no longer in use.
- Changes in classification.
- Assets requiring disposal.
- Assets with outdated or inaccurate records.

Discrepancies shall be documented and assigned for corrective action.

## 13. Exceptions and Non-Compliance

Exceptions require documented business justification, risk assessment and appropriate approval.

Failure to follow this procedure shall be reported to the Information Security Manager and addressed through the organisation's corrective action process where appropriate.

## 14. Records

The following records shall be maintained:

- Information asset inventory.
- Asset assignment records.
- Asset transfer records.
- Asset disposal records.
- Asset review records.
- Asset-related incident records.
- Approved exceptions.

## 15. Procedure Review

This procedure shall be reviewed annually or when significant organisational, technological or legal changes occur.
