## 1. Purpose

This register identifies the interested parties relevant to the Information Security Management System (ISMS) and documents their applicable information security requirements.

## 2. Interested Parties Register

| Interested Party | Requirements and Expectations | How Requirements Are Addressed |
|---|---|---|
| Executive Management | Information security governance, risk visibility and business continuity | Security reporting, risk reviews and management reviews |
| Customers | Confidentiality of customer information, service availability and incident notification | Access controls, encryption, incident management and contractual commitments |
| Employees | Clear security responsibilities, awareness and secure working practices | Security policies, onboarding and awareness training |
| Software Developers | Secure development practices and controlled access to source code | Secure SDLC, code reviews and access restrictions |
| Cloud Service Provider | Compliance with contractual responsibilities and secure cloud configuration | Supplier reviews and shared-responsibility assessment |
| Regulators and Authorities | Compliance with applicable legal and regulatory obligations | Legal register, compliance reviews and assigned responsibilities |
| Payment Service Providers | Secure integration, protection of payment-related information and contractual compliance | Integration security reviews and supplier agreements |
| Business Partners | Protection of shared business information | Confidentiality agreements and controlled information sharing |
| External Auditors | Access to relevant documentation and objective evidence within agreed scope | Document control and evidence management |
| Shareholders | Protection of business information and organisational reputation | Risk management and security governance |

## 3. Legal and Regulatory Considerations

The organisation must identify and evaluate applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

Potential considerations include:

- Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, including applicable amendments and commencement requirements.
- Customer data protection obligations.
- Contractual confidentiality requirements.
- Applicable payment security requirements, where relevant.
- Intellectual property and software licensing obligations.

Applicability must be confirmed against the organisation's actual operations and legal advice where necessary.

## 4. Monitoring and Review

The Information Security Manager is responsible for:

- Reviewing this register annually.
- Identifying new interested parties.
- Monitoring changes to applicable requirements.
- Updating the register when business operations or contractual obligations change.
- Communicating relevant requirements to responsible departments.
