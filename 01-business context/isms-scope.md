

# Information Security Management System (ISMS) Scope

**Document ID:** CL-ISMS-BC-003

**Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document

**Review Frequency:** Annually or following significant organisational changes

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the boundaries and applicability of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) established by CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

The scope is determined considering:

- Internal and external issues affecting information security.
- Requirements of relevant interested parties.
- Organisational activities and services.
- Interfaces and dependencies with external providers.

## 2. ISMS Scope Statement

The ISMS covers the people, processes, information assets, applications and cloud infrastructure involved in the design, development, operation, maintenance and support of the CloudLedger financial management SaaS platform.

The scope includes the organisation's information security management activities at its Colombo headquarters and the AWS cloud environment supporting the SaaS platform.

## 3. Organisational Boundaries

The following departments are included:

- Executive Management.
- Information Technology and Infrastructure.
- Information Security.
- Software Development.
- Finance and Administration.
- Sales and Marketing.
- Customer Support.

All 120 employees are within the organisational scope where their activities support the in-scope services or process in-scope information.

## 4. Physical and Technological Boundaries

### Physical locations

- Colombo headquarters, Sri Lanka.
- Approved remote working locations used by employees.

### Cloud environment

- AWS infrastructure hosting the SaaS platform.
- Cloud databases and storage.
- Application hosting and supporting services.
- Identity and access management services.
- Backup and recovery infrastructure.

## 5. Information Assets Within Scope

- Customer personal information.
- Financial and transaction records.
- Employee information processed by in-scope systems.
- Application source code.
- Authentication and access credentials.
- System configurations and security logs.
- Business continuity and operational records.

## 6. Interfaces and Dependencies

The ISMS recognises dependencies on external organisations, including:

- AWS cloud services.
- Payment processing providers.
- Email and collaboration providers.
- Software development and monitoring platforms.
- External technical support providers.

Security responsibilities shared with these providers will be assessed and managed through supplier security controls and contractual arrangements.

## 7. Exclusions and Justification

No department supporting the in-scope SaaS service is excluded from the ISMS.

The internal infrastructure and operations of external cloud and service providers are not directly controlled by CloudLedger. Their relevant services and shared responsibilities remain subject to supplier assessment, contractual requirements and applicable security oversight.

This does not mean that outsourced activities or associated risks are excluded from the ISMS.

## 8. Scope Review

The Information Security Manager is responsible for reviewing the scope when:

- New products or services are introduced.
- New offices or business units are established.
- Major cloud architecture changes occur.
- New legal or contractual requirements arise.
- Significant security incidents reveal scope gaps.
