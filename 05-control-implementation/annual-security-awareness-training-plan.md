# Annual Security Awareness and Training Plan – 2027

**Document ID:** PLAN-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Organisation:** CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd  
**Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Plan Period:** January–December 2027  
**Related Procedure:** PROC-009 – Information Security Awareness and Training Procedure

## 1. Purpose

This plan establishes the proposed information security awareness and training activities for 2027.

It aims to improve employee understanding of security responsibilities, reduce human-related security risks and ensure that personnel receive training appropriate to their roles.

## 2. Training Objectives

1. Ensure all personnel understand the Information Security Policy.
2. Improve awareness of phishing and social engineering.
3. Promote secure handling of confidential and restricted information.
4. Improve incident identification and reporting.
5. Strengthen secure development and privileged access practices.
6. Evaluate training effectiveness and identify knowledge gaps.

## 3. Annual Training Schedule

| Month | Training Activity | Target Audience | Delivery Method | Effectiveness Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| January | Information Security Policy Awareness | All employees | Online session | Knowledge assessment |
| February | Phishing and Social Engineering | All employees | Workshop | Quiz and reporting awareness |
| March | Password and MFA Security | All employees | Online training | Knowledge assessment |
| April | Data Classification and Handling | All employees | Workshop | Scenario-based assessment |
| May | Incident Reporting and Response | All employees | Tabletop exercise | Exercise evaluation |
| June | Secure Software Development | Developers | Technical workshop | Practical assessment |
| July | Cloud Security Awareness | IT and Cloud Teams | Technical session | Knowledge assessment |
| August | Privileged Access Security | Administrators | Workshop | Scenario-based assessment |
| September | Third-Party and Supplier Security | Procurement and Supplier Owners | Awareness session | Knowledge assessment |
| October | Phishing Awareness Exercise | All employees | Approved simulation | Reporting and interaction metrics |
| November | Information Security Refresher | All employees | Online session | Knowledge assessment |
| December | Annual Awareness Programme Review | Management and Security Team | Review meeting | Programme performance report |

## 4. New Employee Training

New employees must complete the following awareness activities during onboarding:

- Information Security Policy.
- Acceptable Use requirements.
- Password and MFA security.
- Data classification and handling.
- Phishing and social engineering.
- Security incident reporting.

Additional role-specific training must be assigned based on job responsibilities and system access.

## 5. Training Completion Targets

The following are proposed organisational targets:

| Performance Indicator | Proposed Target |
|---|---|
| Annual Mandatory Training Completion | At least 95% |
| New Employee Onboarding Training | 100% within the approved onboarding period |
| Security Awareness Assessment Pass Rate | At least 80% |
| Critical Role-Specific Training Completion | 100% of assigned personnel |
| Overdue Training | Zero overdue assignments after the escalation deadline |

These targets are proposed management objectives, not ISO 27001 requirements.

## 6. Training Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Information Security Manager | Approve and oversee the annual programme |
| HR Manager | Coordinate training schedules and maintain records |
| Department Managers | Ensure employee participation |
| IT Security Team | Deliver technical awareness sessions |
| Employees | Attend training and complete assessments |
| Executive Management | Provide resources and review programme performance |

## 7. Training Evaluation

Training effectiveness will be assessed using:

- Assessment scores.
- Completion rates.
- Employee feedback.
- Phishing simulation results.
- Incident reporting trends.
- Identified security awareness gaps.

Results should be reviewed to determine whether training content, frequency or delivery methods require improvement.

## 8. Resource Requirements

The programme may require:

- Internal security trainers.
- Training materials and presentations.
- Learning management system or tracking spreadsheet.
- Phishing simulation platform, where authorised.
- Employee participation time.
- Management support and training budget.

## 9. Programme Review

The Information Security Manager must review programme performance at the end of the annual period.

The review should identify:

- Completed and outstanding training.
- Performance against approved targets.
- Knowledge gaps.
- Training-related security trends.
- Improvements required for the next annual plan.
