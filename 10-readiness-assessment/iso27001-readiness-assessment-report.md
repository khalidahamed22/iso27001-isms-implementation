## 1. Executive Summary

This report defines the approach for evaluating CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd's readiness for an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification audit.

The assessment is intended to evaluate the documented ISMS framework, risk management arrangements, security policies, control implementation evidence, internal audit activities, management review arrangements, and corrective action processes.

This report is a simulated portfolio artefact. No actual readiness assessment has been conducted, and no certification readiness conclusion has been established.

## 2. Assessment Objectives

The objectives are to:

- Evaluate alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
- Review the completeness of the ISMS documentation.
- Evaluate whether information security risks have been identified and treated.
- Review the Statement of Applicability and control implementation status.
- Identify missing or insufficient implementation evidence.
- Identify outstanding gaps and recommend corrective actions.
- Determine whether further preparation is required before an external certification audit.

## 3. Assessment Scope

The planned assessment covers the ISMS of CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd.

The scope includes:

- ISMS organisational context and scope.
- Leadership and information security responsibilities.
- Risk assessment and risk treatment.
- Information security policies and procedures.
- People, physical, organisational, and technological controls.
- Operational security processes.
- Performance evaluation and internal audit.
- Management review.
- Nonconformity and continual improvement.

## 4. Assessment Criteria

The assessment will use:

1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements, Clauses 4–10.
2. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls, where applicable.
3. CloudLedger's approved ISMS policies and procedures.
4. The organisation's risk assessment methodology and risk treatment plan.
5. The Statement of Applicability.
6. Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and customer requirements identified by the organisation.

## 5. Assessment Methodology

The planned assessment will include:

- Document review.
- Interviews with relevant process and control owners.
- Sampling of operational records.
- Review of control implementation evidence.
- Verification of corrective action records.
- Comparison of documented requirements against actual operational practices.

A control will not be considered implemented merely because a policy or procedure exists. Supporting operational evidence and, where appropriate, effectiveness testing will be required.

## 10. Evidence Evaluation

Evidence should be assessed for:

- Relevance to the requirement or control.
- Completeness.
- Accuracy and authenticity.
- Date and period covered.
- Approval and ownership, where applicable.
- Consistency with actual operational practices.
- Evidence of control operation and effectiveness.

Examples of evidence include system configurations, access review records, training attendance, incident tickets, vulnerability scan reports, backup restoration results, and internal audit records.

Sensitive information should be redacted before inclusion in portfolio materials.

## 11. Readiness Decision

The readiness decision will be completed only after the assessment has been conducted and the findings reviewed.

Possible conclusions include:

- Ready for further certification audit preparation.
- Conditionally ready, subject to closure of identified gaps.
- Not ready, requiring significant corrective action.

**Current Decision:** Not Assessed

No certification readiness claim can be made based on documentation alone.

## 12. Recommended Next Steps

Following the assessment:

1. Complete the readiness checklist.
2. Review the Statement of Applicability against actual risks and requirements.
3. Collect and validate control implementation evidence.
4. Record identified gaps and assign responsible owners.
5. Prioritise corrective actions based on risk.
6. Verify corrective action effectiveness.
7. Conduct the planned internal audit.
8. Conduct management review.
9. Reassess readiness after outstanding gaps have been addressed.
