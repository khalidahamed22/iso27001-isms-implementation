# Internal Audit Procedure

**Document ID:** PROC-011  
**Organisation:** CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd  
**Status:** Draft – Simulated Portfolio Document  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Document Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approval Authority:** Senior Management  
**Review Frequency:** Annually

## 1. Purpose

This procedure establishes the method for planning, conducting, documenting and following up internal audits of the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The objective is to determine whether the ISMS:

- Conforms to CloudLedger's own information security requirements.
- Conforms to applicable requirements of ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
- Is effectively implemented and maintained.
- Achieves its intended information security outcomes.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all processes, departments, systems, locations and activities within the approved ISMS scope.

It includes:

- ISMS governance and business context.
- Information security risk assessment and treatment.
- Policies and procedures.
- Access control and asset management.
- Supplier and cloud security.
- Vulnerability and incident management.
- Business continuity and disaster recovery.
- Awareness and competence.
- Monitoring, measurement and continual improvement.

## 3. Audit Principles

Internal audits shall follow these principles:

- **Integrity:** Auditors shall act honestly and responsibly.
- **Fair presentation:** Audit findings shall accurately reflect available evidence.
- **Due professional care:** Auditors shall exercise appropriate judgement.
- **Confidentiality:** Audit information shall be protected.
- **Evidence-based approach:** Conclusions shall be supported by verifiable evidence.
- **Objectivity:** Audit conclusions shall be impartial and free from bias.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Senior Management | Approve the audit programme and provide resources |
| Information Security Manager | Coordinate the internal audit programme |
| Internal Auditor | Plan and conduct audits, collect evidence and report findings |
| Process Owner | Provide records, explanations and access to relevant evidence |
| ISMS Manager | Coordinate corrective action activities |
| Audit Reviewer | Review audit reports for completeness and accuracy |

Auditors shall not audit their own work where this would compromise objectivity and impartiality.

Where internal resources cannot provide sufficient independence or competence, management may consider an appropriately qualified external auditor.

## 5. Audit Programme

The organisation shall establish an internal audit programme considering:

- Process importance.
- Information security risks.
- Changes affecting the organisation.
- Results of previous audits.
- Significant incidents or control weaknesses.
- Relevant contractual and regulatory requirements.

The programme shall define audit frequency, methods, responsibilities, planning requirements and reporting arrangements.

## 6. Audit Planning

Before conducting an audit, the auditor shall prepare an audit plan containing:

| Planning Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Audit Reference | Unique audit identifier |
| Audit Objectives | What the audit intends to establish |
| Audit Scope | Processes, locations and systems covered |
| Audit Criteria | ISO clauses, Annex A controls and internal requirements |
| Audit Method | Interviews, document review, sampling and observation |
| Audit Team | Assigned auditor or auditors |
| Audit Schedule | Planned dates and activities |
| Process Owners | Relevant personnel |
| Evidence Requirements | Records and information required |

The audit plan shall be communicated to relevant process owners before the audit.

## 7. Audit Execution

Auditors may use the following methods:

### 7.1 Document Review

Review documented policies, procedures, risk assessments, registers, plans and records.

### 7.2 Interviews

Interview relevant personnel to establish their understanding of responsibilities and implemented processes.

### 7.3 Observation

Observe relevant operational activities where access and authorisation are available.

### 7.4 Evidence Sampling

Select appropriate samples of records, transactions, access requests, incidents, supplier assessments or other evidence.

### 7.5 Technical Verification

Where required and authorised, examine technical configurations, system logs, security settings and relevant monitoring records.

Documentary evidence alone shall not be treated as proof of technical control effectiveness where technical verification is necessary.

## 8. Audit Findings

Audit findings shall be classified using the following internal classification scheme:

| Classification | Description |
|---|---|
| Major Nonconformity | Significant failure affecting the ability of the ISMS to achieve intended outcomes or meet an important requirement |
| Minor Nonconformity | An isolated failure to meet a requirement that does not indicate a broader systemic breakdown |
| Observation | An issue or weakness that warrants attention but is not established as a nonconformity |
| Opportunity for Improvement | A recommendation to improve an existing process or control |

These classifications are the organisation's proposed internal reporting categories, not mandatory ISO terminology.

Each finding shall include:

- Finding reference.
- Applicable requirement or audit criterion.
- Objective evidence.
- Description of the issue.
- Classification.
- Responsible process owner.
- Required follow-up.

## 9. Audit Reporting

An audit report shall be prepared after completion of the audit.

The report shall include:

- Audit objectives and scope.
- Audit criteria.
- Audit dates and participants.
- Audit methods and evidence reviewed.
- Summary of audit results.
- Nonconformities and observations.
- Overall audit conclusion.
- Corrective action requirements.

Audit reports shall be communicated to relevant management and process owners.

## 10. Corrective Action and Follow-Up

Nonconformities shall be addressed through the corrective action process.

The responsible process owner shall:

1. Correct the identified nonconformity where appropriate.
2. Determine its root cause.
3. Evaluate whether similar issues exist elsewhere.
4. Implement necessary corrective actions.
5. Retain documented evidence.
6. Support verification of action effectiveness.

Auditors or authorised reviewers shall verify corrective action completion and effectiveness.

## 11. Auditor Competence and Independence

Internal auditors shall have appropriate knowledge and competence relating to:

- Audit principles and techniques.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
- Information security management.
- Risk-based auditing.
- Evidence collection and evaluation.
- Nonconformity identification and reporting.

Audit assignments shall consider conflicts of interest and independence.

## 12. Documented Information

The following records shall be retained:

- Internal audit programme.
- Individual audit plans.
- Audit checklists.
- Audit working papers and evidence references.
- Audit reports.
- Nonconformity records.
- Corrective action records.
- Follow-up and effectiveness verification records.

Access to audit records shall be restricted to authorised personnel.

## 13. Review and Continual Improvement

The internal audit programme shall be reviewed periodically to determine whether audit coverage, frequency and methods remain appropriate.

Lessons learned and audit results shall be considered during management review and ISMS improvement activities.
