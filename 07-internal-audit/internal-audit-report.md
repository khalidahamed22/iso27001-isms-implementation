## 1. Executive Summary

This report template is intended to document the results of the internal audit of CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd's information security risk assessment and treatment processes.

The audit is designed to evaluate conformity with relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements and the organisation's internal risk management procedures.

**Audit Status:** Planned – Not Conducted.

No audit findings or conclusions have been established because the planned audit activities have not been performed.

## 2. Audit Objectives

The audit objectives are to evaluate whether:

- A documented risk assessment methodology is established.
- Information security risks are identified and assigned to risk owners.
- Risks are analysed and evaluated consistently.
- Risk treatment decisions are documented.
- The Statement of Applicability is appropriately justified.
- Risk treatment implementation is supported by evidence.
- Residual risks are assessed and formally accepted where appropriate.

## 3. Audit Scope

The audit covers:

- Risk Assessment Methodology.
- Asset Register.
- Information Security Risk Register.
- Risk Treatment Plan.
- Statement of Applicability.
- Risk ownership and approval records.
- Risk treatment implementation evidence.
- Residual risk assessment and acceptance records.

The audit applies to the simulated CloudLedger ISMS scope, including its SaaS platform, AWS cloud environment, supporting business processes and relevant third-party dependencies.

The planned audit methodology includes:

1. Review of documented ISMS information.
2. Interviews with relevant risk owners and process owners.
3. Risk-based sampling of the risk register.
4. Recalculation of selected risk scores.
5. Verification of selected treatment actions.
6. Examination of residual risk acceptance records.
7. Evaluation of evidence against audit criteria.
