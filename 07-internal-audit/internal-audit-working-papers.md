## 4. Planned Audit Test Procedures

### WP-001 – Risk Assessment Methodology

**Objective:** Determine whether a documented and consistent risk assessment methodology exists.

**Planned Procedures:**
1. Obtain the current risk assessment methodology.
2. Review likelihood and impact definitions.
3. Examine risk acceptance criteria.
4. Check whether the methodology explains how risks are evaluated.
5. Compare selected risk register entries against the methodology.

**Evidence Expected:** Risk Assessment Methodology and Risk Register.

**Current Status:** Not Performed.

### WP-002 – Risk Identification

**Objective:** Determine whether relevant information security risks have been identified.

**Planned Procedures:**
1. Obtain the current asset register and risk register.
2. Select a sample of information assets.
3. Trace selected assets to associated risks.
4. Examine whether confidentiality, integrity and availability impacts are considered.
5. Review whether risk owners are assigned.
6. Examine supplier and cloud-related risks.

**Evidence Expected:** Asset Register, Risk Register and supplier risk records.

**Current Status:** Not Performed.

### WP-003 – Risk Analysis and Evaluation

**Objective:** Determine whether identified risks are analysed and evaluated consistently.

**Planned Procedures:**
1. Select a sample of risk entries.
2. Compare likelihood ratings with documented definitions.
3. Compare impact ratings with documented definitions.
4. Independently recalculate likelihood × impact scores.
5. Compare calculated scores against recorded scores.
6. Verify that risk levels correspond to the organisation's thresholds.
7. Examine how risks are prioritised.

**Evidence Expected:** Risk Register and Risk Assessment Methodology.

**Current Status:** Not Performed.

### WP-004 – Risk Treatment

**Objective:** Determine whether risk treatment decisions and control selections are appropriately documented.

**Planned Procedures:**
1. Obtain the Risk Treatment Plan.
2. Select a sample of high and critical risks.
3. Examine selected treatment options.
4. Trace proposed controls to the Statement of Applicability.
5. Review applicability justifications.
6. Check treatment owners and target dates.
7. Where implementation is claimed, inspect supporting evidence.

**Evidence Expected:** Risk Treatment Plan, SoA and implementation records.

**Current Status:** Not Performed.

### WP-005 – Residual Risk and Approval

**Objective:** Determine whether residual risks are assessed and accepted appropriately.

**Planned Procedures:**
1. Identify risks for which treatment is reported as complete.
2. Request evidence that the relevant controls were implemented.
3. Examine evidence of control effectiveness.
4. Review residual risk calculations.
5. Verify risk owner review and acceptance records.
6. Confirm that proposed or unverified controls are not incorrectly treated as effective.

**Evidence Expected:** Risk acceptance records, control testing records and residual risk assessments.

**Current Status:** Not Performed.
