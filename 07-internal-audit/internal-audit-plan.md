## 1. Audit Purpose

The purpose of this audit is to evaluate whether CloudLedger's information security risk assessment and treatment processes conform to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements and the organisation's documented procedures.

The audit will examine whether risks are consistently identified, analysed, evaluated, treated and monitored.

## 2. Audit Objectives

The audit aims to determine whether:

1. Risk assessment criteria and methodology are established.
2. Information security risks are identified within the ISMS scope.
3. Risk owners are assigned.
4. Likelihood and impact assessments follow the approved methodology.
5. Risks are evaluated against established acceptance criteria.
6. Risk treatment options are selected and documented.
7. The Statement of Applicability is appropriately justified.
8. Risk treatment plans are approved by relevant authorities.
9. Residual risks are assessed after treatment implementation.
10. Risk assessment records are reviewed when significant changes occur.

## 3. Audit Scope

The audit covers the following documents and activities:

- Risk Assessment Methodology.
- Asset Register.
- Information Security Risk Register.
- Risk Treatment Plan.
- Statement of Applicability.
- Risk ownership and approval arrangements.
- Risk treatment evidence.
- Residual risk assessment and acceptance records.

**Organisational Scope:** CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd ISMS.

## 6. Audit Methodology

The audit will use the following methods:

### 6.1 Document Review

Inspect the risk methodology, asset register, risk register, treatment plan and SoA.

### 6.2 Interviews

Discuss risk identification, scoring, treatment and approval practices with relevant risk owners.

### 6.3 Sampling

Select a sample of risks from the risk register, including:

- Critical risks.
- High risks.
- Risks involving customer financial information.
- Cloud infrastructure risks.
- Supplier-related risks.
- Risks with proposed or completed treatment actions.

### 6.4 Evidence Verification

Compare documented risk treatments with available implementation evidence.

A proposed treatment shall not be treated as implemented merely because it appears in a treatment plan.

Residual risk shall not be accepted as verified unless treatment implementation and effectiveness have been supported by appropriate evidence.
