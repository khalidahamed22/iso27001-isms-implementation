**Organisation:** CloudLedger Technologies (Pvt) Ltd

**Classification:** Internal

**Programme Period:** January–December 2027  
**Approval Authority:** Senior Management

## 1. Purpose

This Annual Internal Audit Programme establishes the planned audit activities for evaluating the conformity and effectiveness of CloudLedger's Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The programme uses a risk-based approach, prioritising areas according to business importance, information security risks, previous findings, organisational changes and applicable requirements.

## 2. Audit Programme Objectives

The objectives are to:

- Evaluate conformity with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
- Assess whether internal ISMS policies and procedures are followed.
- Evaluate the implementation and effectiveness of selected security controls.
- Identify nonconformities and improvement opportunities.
- Assess the effectiveness of risk treatment activities.
- Provide management with information for continual improvement.

## 3. Audit Planning Considerations

The following factors will influence audit priorities:

| Factor | Consideration |
|---|---|
| Risk Level | Critical and high-risk areas receive greater attention |
| Business Criticality | Processes supporting financial information and SaaS availability receive priority |
| Previous Findings | Areas with unresolved findings receive follow-up attention |
| Organisational Changes | New systems, suppliers or business processes may require additional audits |
| Security Incidents | Significant incidents may trigger focused audit activities |
| Regulatory and Contractual Requirements | Relevant obligations are included in audit criteria |

## 5. Audit Frequency and Risk Prioritisation

The proposed programme gives priority to critical business processes and higher-risk areas.

Additional audits may be scheduled when:

- A significant security incident occurs.
- Important organisational or technology changes take place.
- New suppliers introduce significant risks.
- Previous audit findings remain unresolved.
- Management requests an independent assessment of a specific area.

The schedule may be adjusted based on emerging risks and available audit resources.

## 6. Audit Resources

The following resources are expected to support the programme:

- Competent internal auditors.
- Access to relevant policies and procedures.
- Risk registers and Statements of Applicability.
- Asset and supplier registers.
- Security and operational records.
- Relevant system owners and process owners.
- Appropriate technical access where authorised.

Auditors shall maintain impartiality and shall not audit their own work where this compromises objectivity.

## 7. Audit Methods

The programme may use:

1. Document and record inspection.
2. Personnel interviews.
3. Process walkthroughs.
4. Sampling of operational records.
5. Observation of relevant activities.
6. Technical verification where authorised and appropriate.

The selected methods shall be appropriate to the audit objectives and risks.

## 8. Reporting and Follow-Up

Each audit shall produce a documented report containing:

- Audit objectives and scope.
- Audit criteria.
- Audit methods.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Findings and supporting evidence.
- Audit conclusions.
- Required corrective actions.

Nonconformities shall be tracked through the corrective action process.

## 9. Programme Monitoring

The Information Security Manager shall monitor:

| Performance Indicator | Proposed Target |
|---|---|
| Planned audits completed | 100% of approved programme, subject to authorised changes |
| Audit reports issued | Within 10 working days of each audit |
| Findings assigned to owners | 100% of recorded nonconformities |
| Corrective actions tracked | 100% of recorded nonconformities |
| Follow-up verification | Completed according to approved action deadlines |

These are proposed internal performance targets, not ISO-mandated requirements.

## 10. Programme Review

The annual audit programme shall be reviewed:

- During management review.
- Following significant organisational changes.
- After major information security incidents.
- When new significant risks are identified.
- When audit resources or priorities change.

Changes to the programme shall be documented and approved by the appropriate authority.
