## 8. Further Actions

If the corrective action is ineffective or partially effective, the responsible owner shall:

1. Reopen the corrective action.
2. Investigate why the previous action was insufficient.
3. Reassess the root cause.
4. Develop additional corrective actions.
5. Assign new responsibilities and target dates.
6. Arrange another effectiveness verification.

## 9. Closure Decision

A corrective action may be closed only when:

- The implementation has been completed.
- Sufficient objective evidence has been reviewed.
- The root cause has been addressed.
- Effectiveness has been evaluated.
- Any required further actions have been addressed.
- Closure has been authorised.
