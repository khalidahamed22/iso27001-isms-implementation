## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the management review is to enable senior management to evaluate the continuing suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the Information Security Management System (ISMS).

The review will assess ISMS performance, identify areas requiring improvement and determine whether changes to the ISMS are necessary.

## 2. Participants

| Participant | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | Chair and management decisions |
| Information Security Manager | Present ISMS performance |
| IT Manager | Present technical security performance |
| HR Manager | Present employee awareness and competence |
| Compliance Representative | Present legal and regulatory requirements |
| Risk Owners | Present significant risk updates |

## 3. Management Review Agenda

### 3.1 Status of Previous Management Review Actions

Review outstanding actions from previous management reviews.

**Information Required:**
- Previous meeting minutes.
- Outstanding action register.
- Completed and overdue actions.
- Evidence supporting action closure.

### 3.2 Changes in External and Internal Issues

Review changes that may affect the ISMS.

**Information Required:**
- Business strategy changes.
- Technology and infrastructure changes.
- New or changed threats.
- Organisational restructuring.
- Regulatory and contractual changes.
- Changes to cloud services and suppliers.

### 3.3 Changes in Interested Parties' Needs and Expectations

Review whether relevant interested-party requirements have changed.

**Information Required:**
- Customer security requirements.
- Supplier contractual obligations.
- Applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
- Employee and management expectations.
- Relevant stakeholder feedback.

### 3.4 ISMS Performance and Effectiveness

Evaluate whether the ISMS is achieving its intended results.

**Information Required:**
- Information security objectives and KPI results.
- Internal audit results.
- Risk assessment and treatment status.
- Nonconformities and corrective actions.
- Security incident statistics.
- Vulnerability management performance.
- Access review results.
- Supplier security review results.
- Backup and recovery test results.
- Awareness and training effectiveness.

### 3.5 Customer and Stakeholder Feedback

Review feedback relating to information security.

**Information Required:**
- Customer complaints.
- Security-related customer requests.
- Supplier feedback.
- Contractual security review outcomes.
- Stakeholder concerns.

### 3.6 Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment Status

Review significant information security risks and treatment progress.

**Information Required:**
- Updated risk register.
- Critical and high-risk items.
- Risk treatment plan.
- Residual risk assessments.
- Risk acceptance decisions.
- Changes in risk exposure.

### 3.7 Opportunities for Continual Improvement

Identify opportunities to improve the ISMS.

**Information Required:**
- Audit improvement opportunities.
- Control weaknesses.
- Process inefficiencies.
- Security automation opportunities.
- Training and awareness improvements.
- Technology and monitoring improvements.

### 3.8 Resource Adequacy

Evaluate whether sufficient resources are available to operate and improve the ISMS.

**Information Required:**
- Security staffing.
- Training budget.
- Security technology requirements.
- Infrastructure resources.
- Audit and compliance resources.
- Management support requirements.

## 4. Expected Management Review Outputs

Management decisions and actions should address:

1. Opportunities for continual improvement.
2. Any need for changes to the ISMS.
3. Resource requirements.
4. Changes to security objectives or priorities.
5. Risk treatment and acceptance decisions requiring management attention.
6. Assigned responsibilities and target completion dates.
